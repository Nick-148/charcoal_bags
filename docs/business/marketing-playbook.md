# Marketing playbook: zero ad spend

Goal: 50–100 bags/month in months 1–3, mostly from campus word of mouth and short-form video, on
about 3 hours a week of content time.

## The growth loop

```
Satisfying / funny short video ──► views ──► profile ──► store link
        ▲                                                   │
        │                                                   ▼
  UGC + duets ◄── buyers post their desk setups ◄── order ships
        │           (a "field report" card in every box asks them to)
        └──► campus reps and teams buy in bulk ──► more desks, more lockers, more video
```

Every package includes a small card: "Field report requested. Post your setup and tag
@blackbagodor." Repost the best ones. That's the free supply of social proof. Never invent reviews.

## Content pillars (rotate them)

1. **Satisfying build:** stitching, the pour of charcoal, sealing the bag. Silent and ASMR-friendly.
   15–25 s.
2. **Threat assessment:** a deadpan "field report" on the stinkiest gear (hockey bag, cleats, wrestling
   shoes), deploy a bag overnight, show the result. Keep claims honest: "smells like nothing now".
3. **Desk cornhole:** trick shots into a mug, office or dorm tournaments, "1v1 your roommate".
4. **Recharge ritual:** the monthly sunbath as a calm, aesthetic loop.
5. **Founder log:** batch numbers, pre-order progress, packing orders. People buy from people.

## 4-week launch calendar (3 posts/week, TikTok + Reels + Shorts from one edit)

| Week | Mon                         | Wed                             | Fri                                       |
| ---- | --------------------------- | ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| 1    | Build ASMR: Batch 01 begins | "Your gym bag is a crime scene" | Founder's Pre-Order open (link in bio)    |
| 2    | Desk cornhole trick shot    | Cleat overnight test            | Pre-order counter ("14/25 Blackout left") |
| 3    | Dorm hall tournament        | Recharge ritual                 | Team order unboxing (bulk)                |
| 4    | Packing Batch 01 orders     | Customer field report repost    | "Batch 02 colorway vote" poll             |

Hooks that work for this product: an absurd claim followed by a demo ("this bag is the only thing
keeping my roommate alive"), a POV ("POV: your lacrosse bag after preseason"), or a challenge
("make the cup: win a bag").

## Campus playbook

1. **Teams and clubs:** DM club sports captains (lacrosse, hockey, rugby, wrestling) offering team
   pricing (10+ bags at $7.50) and custom team colors through the quote form.
2. **Dorm RAs and floor events:** donate a desk-cornhole set for a floor tournament in exchange for
   posts.
3. **Campus reps (month 2+):** one per campus. They get a referral code (roadmap: Stripe promotion
   codes) plus free bags.
4. **Pop-ups:** set up a table at a game-day tailgate or the student center with a cornhole board
   and "make it, get 20% off". Sell from pre-made stock.

## Tailgate and backyard-game crossover

Post in cornhole communities and at tailgates. The product is native to that world, and home-goods
brands ignore it. Pitch it as the stocking stuffer for the cornhole person in your life.

## Metrics (check weekly)

- Store: sessions (Cloudflare Web Analytics), cart-to-checkout rate, conversion, average bags per
  order, share of orders at bulk tiers.
- Content: views per post, profile visits, link clicks. Double down on the top pillar every two weeks.
- Ops: pre-order slots sold per batch, days from order to ship.

## Budget guardrails

- $0 ads until organic content proves a hook (a post with over 50k views that also drove sales).
- First paid test, month 3+: $50 Spark Ads boosting the best organic post, never new creative.
